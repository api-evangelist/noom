What you’ll learn: If you’ve been reading about GLP-1 medications, you probably know that GLP-1 stands for glucagon-like peptide-1, a hormone involved in blood sugar regulation and appetite. Medications like Wegovy® and Ozempic® mimic the effects of GLP-1, while tirzepatide—the active ingredient in Zepbound® and Mounjaro®—targets two hormones: GLP-1 and GIP. […]
