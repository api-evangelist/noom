What you’ll learn: People can lose a lot of weight on Zepbound® (tirzepatide) and Wegovy® (semaglutide). The catch is what happens if you stop taking the medication. In one ⁠study, people who stopped their weekly semaglutide shot gained back about two-thirds of the weight they’d lost within a year.
