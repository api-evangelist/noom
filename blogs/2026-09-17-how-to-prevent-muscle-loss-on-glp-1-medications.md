What you’ll learn: You may have heard that GLP-1 medications cause muscle loss. If you’re taking Wegovy® (semaglutide) or Zepbound® (tirzepatide), that can sound alarming. After all, you want to lose fat—not the muscle that helps keep you strong, active, and healthy.
