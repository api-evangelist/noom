Grits have been a Southern breakfast staple for generations, but lately they’re showing up in a new way. Across social media, people are transforming them into protein- and fiber-rich breakfast bowls by topping them with eggs, cottage cheese, chicken sausage, vegetables, and other nutrient-dense ingredients. That shift toward higher-protein, higher-fiber toppings is a smart upgrade.
