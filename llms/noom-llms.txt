# Noom

> Noom, Inc. is a New York-based digital health company (founded 2008 by Saeju Jeong and Artem Petakov) whose psychology-based weight-management app pairs behaviour-change coursework and human coaching with food and activity logging, and — through Noom Med — clinician-prescribed GLP-1 medication. Noom also sells to employers, health plans and health systems. **Noom publishes no public API, no developer portal, no OpenAPI/GraphQL contract, and no client SDKs.** Its application host `api.noom.com` answers every anonymous request with HTTP 401 UNAUTHENTICATED.
